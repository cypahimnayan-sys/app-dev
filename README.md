# *Stranger Things* (2016) — My Favorite Series

## **Favorite Line**
> “Friends don’t lie.”  

---

## **Overview**
**Stranger Things** is a hit **science fiction horror series** created by *The Duffer Brothers* and released on **Netflix** in 2016.  
The story mixes mystery, friendship, and adventure in a small town full of strange secrets.  

**Main Cast:**  
- **Millie Bobby Brown** as *Eleven*, a quiet girl with powerful abilities  
- **Finn Wolfhard**, **Gaten Matarazzo**, and **Caleb McLaughlin** as her loyal friends  
- **David Harbour** as *Chief Hopper*, the town’s police officer who helps solve the mystery  

---

## **Story Summary**
The story starts when a young boy named *Will Byers* suddenly disappears in Hawkins, Indiana.  
While his friends search for him, they meet *Eleven*, a mysterious girl with telekinetic powers who escaped from a secret lab.  
As they search for Will, they uncover the truth about the **Upside Down**, a terrifying parallel world.  

---

## **Themes**
1. **Friendship and Loyalty** — sticking together even when things get scary  
2. **Courage in Fear** — learning to be brave even when you are afraid  
3. **Growing Up** — dealing with change, loss, and responsibility  

---

## **Did You Know?**
- The show’s 1980s setting was inspired by classic sci-fi and horror films like *E.T.* and *The Goonies*.  
- Many of the actors were teenagers when the show became popular worldwide.  

---

## **Why I Love It**
I love *Stranger Things* because it has everything I enjoy — suspense, heart, and friendship.  
It’s not only about monsters but also about how people support each other through hard times.  
Every season makes me feel excited, scared, and inspired all at once.

# *K-Pop: Demon Hunters* (2025) — My Favorite Animated Movie

## **Favorite Line**
> “The spotlight can fade, but courage never does.”  

---

## **Overview**
**K-Pop: Demon Hunters** is a **2025 animated action-fantasy movie** produced by *Sony Pictures Animation* and released on **Netflix**.  
It tells the story of a famous K-pop girl group who secretly live double lives as demon hunters.  
The movie combines music, Korean culture, and fantasy adventure with a strong message about bravery and teamwork.  

**Main Characters:**  
- **Rumi** — the fearless leader of the group and the main heroine (voiced by *Arden Cho*)  
- **Mira** — the tech genius and strategist of the team (voiced by *May Hong*)  
- **Zoey** — the cheerful and energetic dancer (voiced by *Ji-young Yoo*)  
- **Jinu** — a former idol who becomes their rival and the main villain (voiced by *Ahn Hyo-seop*)  

---

## **Story Summary**
The story follows Rumi and her idol group who perform for millions of fans by day but secretly fight demons by night.  
When dark spirits threaten Seoul, the girls must use their talent, courage, and bond to protect the city while hiding their true identities.  
It’s a fast-paced story about friendship, strength, and believing in yourself.  

---

## **Themes**
1. **Identity** — being true to yourself even under pressure  
2. **Teamwork** — fighting stronger when everyone works together  
3. **Courage** — using your gifts to protect others  

---

## **Did You Know?**
- *K-Pop: Demon Hunters* is the **first Korean-inspired animated movie** from *Sony Pictures Animation*.  
- The film features original songs and designs inspired by real K-pop performances.  
- It was officially released on **June 20, 2025**, and quickly gained fans around the world.  

---

## **Why I Love It**
I love *K-Pop: Demon Hunters* because it shows that music and passion can be powerful weapons for good.  
It’s full of color, energy, and heart, and I like how the characters fight not just monsters but also their own fears.  
It’s inspiring to see a movie that celebrates Korean culture while delivering a fun and meaningful story.
